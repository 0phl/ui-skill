## What this batch adds

**Editorial composition has a larger role.** The two property pages use expansive photographs, offset text columns, thin rules and mostly rectilinear controls. The food page combines serif headlines, occasional script accents, illustration, photography and a strong editorial grid. Subly pairs a serif hero with sans-serif interface text. These references broaden the available directions; they do not establish a universal font or a new preference for a particular brand style.

**Clean controls can sit inside expressive pages.** The sports-club cards use playful character illustrations with a predictable age/title/description/action anatomy. Paceform uses photography and translucent previews around a clear hero message and action. The agriculture and club pages repeat local controls across different section layouts. The reusable quality is the relationship between content and controls, with decoration chosen for the project.

**Long pages need deliberate pacing.** Subly supplies a full sequence from hero and product preview through features, supporting evidence, pricing, an estimator and the footer. The property and food montages show alternatives to repeating one card grid throughout. Larger gaps separate ideas; tighter groups connect a message with its image, metadata and action. This is a composition inference, not a required section checklist.

**Context and task can stay beside one another.** HealthRate separates identity, evaluation information and appointment choices into three regions. Orqenly separates navigation, publishing/feed work and secondary insights. Each puts the main task's action inside its own context. Neither screenshot proves sticky positioning or behavior at other widths.

**The CloudNexus group shows consistent overview and detail anatomy.** A resource table, project list, deployment overview, selected row, failure detail and isolated components repeat icon scale, text tiers, spacing and status treatments. Page actions, table filters, exception recovery, detail actions and recommendation review are visually distinct in scope. Failure explanations and a disabled action's reason sit close to the affected controls. These are visible presentation patterns; operational rules still have to come from the actual product.

## Applying the four confirmed priorities

**Icons:** the operational examples use small coherent utility symbols with regular icon/label gaps. The editorial pages use fewer functional icons, often arrows near an action or play controls over media. Brand marks, cartoon characters, food cutouts and large photographic subjects have separate roles. A coherent interface can contain both simple utility icons and expressive imagery.

**Spacing:** keep compact relationships within a row, field group, card or message/action unit. Use larger separation between sections or task regions. The property examples support broad editorial whitespace; the cloud and campaign examples support compact, aligned data. No exact spacing scale or density setting has been extracted from these screenshots.

**Button placement:** place header actions with page context, table controls above the affected data, item controls at the item, and detail actions beside the selected object's identity. Booking submission follows date/time choices; publishing sits inside the composer. Marketing actions accompany the corresponding message. The evidence supports contextual placement, with no universal screen corner, button shape or color.

**Overall appearance:** select a relevant composition for the task. A photographic public page, playful discovery rail, provider profile, social workspace and operations table can share clarity while differing in type, density and geometry. Carry their useful relationships into the requested client theme.
